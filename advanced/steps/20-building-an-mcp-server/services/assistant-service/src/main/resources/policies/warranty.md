# Warranty

Every product has a 2-year warranty from the delivery date against defects in materials and workmanship.
Lamps and other electrical products have a 3-year warranty.
The warranty does not cover normal wear, accidents, or damage from using a product in a way it was not made for.
To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a photo of the defect.
ShopLite repairs or replaces the product; if neither is possible, you get a full refund.
