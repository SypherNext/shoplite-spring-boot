# Shipping

Orders placed before 14:00 Central European Time on a working day leave the warehouse the same day.
Standard delivery inside the EU takes 2 to 4 working days and costs 4.90 EUR. It is free for orders over 50 EUR.
Express delivery takes 1 working day inside Germany, Austria and the Netherlands and costs 9.90 EUR.
ShopLite ships to the EU, Switzerland, Norway and the United Kingdom. Customs fees outside the EU are paid by the customer.
Every parcel has a tracking number, sent by email when the parcel leaves the warehouse.
