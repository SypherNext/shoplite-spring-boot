# Payments

ShopLite accepts credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard) and bank transfer. Prices are in EUR and include VAT.
Your card is charged when you place the order. If the payment is declined, the order stays in PAYMENT_FAILED and no stock is kept for it.
ShopLite never stores full card numbers: the payment provider handles card data.
Invoices are available as PDF on the order page once the order is paid.
